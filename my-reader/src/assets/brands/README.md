# Cloud provider artwork

These assets identify the corresponding integrations. They remain the trademarks
of their respective owners and are not covered by the repository's MIT license.
Both apps bundle identical copies and preserve the original artwork and colours.

- `google-drive-2026.png`: unmodified 128 px PNG linked by the
  [Google Drive branding guide](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/branding).
  [Official asset](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/drive_2026/v2/web-64dp/logo_drive_2026_color_2x_web_64dp.png),
  retrieved 2026-11-11. The guide permits use in Drive integrations and resizing,
  but no other modifications. Google Drive is a trademark of Google LLC.
- `dropbox-glyph-128.png`: unmodified `Glyph/Glyph_128.png` from the
  [official brand partner toolkit](https://brand.dropbox.com/logo), retrieved
  2026-11-11. The
  [developer branding guide](https://docs.dropboxapi.com/dropbox-api/docs/developer-resources/branding-guide)
  permits the approved logo to identify Dropbox functionality. Preserve the
  supplied blue and clear space. Dropbox is a trademark of Dropbox, Inc.

Mobile copies live in `my-reader-mobile/assets/images/providers/`.
S3-compatible storage uses a generic storage symbol with the app's semantic
accent colour. The [AWS icon guidelines](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/)
describe architecture diagrams and documentation; this integration also covers
non-AWS services.
